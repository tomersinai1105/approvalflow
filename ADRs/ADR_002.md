# ADR 0002: Use a deterministic router instead of letting the AI make the final approval decision

## Context

The system uses an AI classifier to evaluate invoices against the expense policy. However, the AI model can be uncertain, may misclassify an invoice, or may be influenced by malicious text in the invoice payload.

The system must prove that it never auto-approves above the configured autonomy ceiling.

## Decision

The AI classifier is used only as a decision-support component.

The final route is decided by a deterministic router in the Workflow Service.

The router checks:

- input validation result
- duplicate status
- amount in USD
- configured autonomy ceiling
- configured confidence threshold
- preprocess hard stops
- payload-instruction signal
- policy violations
- AI recommendation and confidence

The system can auto-approve only when all deterministic safety checks pass.

## Consequences

This makes the system safer because the AI cannot approve an invoice by itself.

Even if the AI recommends approval, the router can block auto-approval when the invoice is above the autonomy ceiling, has low confidence, contains a hard stop, or violates policy.

The trade-off is that some invoices may be escalated to human review even when the AI thinks they are probably safe. This is acceptable because the project prioritizes safe automation over risky automation.
